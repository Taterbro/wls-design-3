# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS

## Users

Seven institutional audiences, weighted equally — this is one corporate gateway, not a single-audience funnel:

- **Investors / family offices** — need credible strategy, opportunities, governance and pipeline logic; primary conversion is an investment discussion or approved data access.
- **Governments / public agencies** — need delivery capability, sectors and PPP/JV approach; primary conversion is a strategic meeting.
- **Corporates / technology firms** — need market access and local execution; primary conversion is proposing a partnership.
- **Financial institutions / DFIs** — need bankability, governance, ESG and project structure; primary conversion is a financing discussion.
- **Project owners** — need origination and development capability; primary conversion is submitting a project.
- **Talent / experts** — need vision, culture, sectors and global ambition; primary conversion is careers / expert network.
- **Media / researchers** — need verified facts, leadership, releases and insights; primary conversion is a media enquiry or media-kit download.

Operating situation: senior external decision-makers evaluating whether to engage a group they do not yet know, from markets inside and outside Africa.

## Product Purpose

Present Waterlight Group as an African-founded global enterprise platform that builds, invests in and scales businesses, infrastructure and technologies addressing essential economic and human needs. The website must function simultaneously as corporate headquarters, investment gateway, partnership engine, project showcase, thought-leadership platform and trust centre. Success means a visiting decision-maker concludes the group is credible and sees a serious opportunity to work together.

## Positioning

An African-founded global enterprise group that originates, structures, invests in and builds across essential sectors — investment, industry, innovation, infrastructure and impact. The mechanism a neighbor could not truthfully copy is the African origin paired with a genuinely global mandate and operating architecture: "From Africa to the World. From the World to Africa."

## Operating Context

- The site is the primary corporate and business-development surface; it will evolve into a controlled deal-origination platform.
- Public visitors see approved project summaries; qualified investors may request controlled access to restricted material.
- Publishing is governed: content is managed through a CMS with role-based publishing and approval workflow, and enquiries are routed through a CRM.
- A single master site explaining a multi-entity group without implying unestablished legal relationships.
- Multilingual-ready: English at launch; Arabic and French planned without redesign.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed scope and structure:

- Full corporate site: Home; About (Our Story, Vision & Mission, Group Structure, Leadership, Governance); eight business platforms; four companies; Investments; Projects; Innovation (WSWIS); Global Markets; Impact; Insights; Leadership; Partner With Us; Careers; Contact / Global Offices; Legal.
- Eight priority business platforms: Water & Smart Infrastructure; Renewable Energy & Integrated Utilities; Agriculture & Food Systems; Technology, AI & Digital Infrastructure; Industrial Development & Manufacturing; Real Estate, Housing & Urban Infrastructure; Logistics & Supply Chains; Climate & ESG Solutions.
- Group brand architecture to present clearly: Waterlight Group; Waterlight Africa Investments Ltd; Waterlight Industries Limited; Waterlight iHEAL Global Limited; Waterlight Limited; and Waterlight Save Initiative (presented separately as an independent nonprofit/development organization, not a commercial subsidiary).
- Role-based "Build With Waterlight" enquiry routes (Investor, Government, Financial Institution, Technology Partner, Project Owner, Joint Venture, Africa Market Entry, GCC Market Entry) with route-specific qualification fields.
- Project records carry public fields: identity, commercial, development stage, opportunity type, impact where verified, evidence where publishable, and action.
- Global Markets uses four distinct footprint statuses: Established Operations, Active Partnerships, Projects / Opportunities, Strategic Target Markets.
- Flagship innovation product: WSWIS — intelligent water infrastructure (solar energy → source/treatment → smart storage → intelligent distribution → digital monitoring → predictive maintenance → reliable access).

Constraints:

- No confidential investment document may be exposed through an ordinary public URL; restricted material requires authentication, permissioning, audit logs and approval workflows.
- Legal entity descriptions and relationships must be validated before launch and must not imply unestablished legal relationships.
- No unsupported claim of office, investment, partnership or market presence; target markets must be visually distinguished from actual footprint.
- The nonprofit identity (Waterlight Save Initiative, including any 501(c)(3) / UN ECOSOC wording) must be published only with current supporting documentation.

Explicitly undecided:

- Deploy target / hosting for the static build.
- CMS platform and CRM integration.
- Operational owner and access policy for the secure investor/document area.
- Final verified legal entity chart and jurisdictions.

## Product Principles

1. **Evidence before ambition** — never publish an unverified metric, partnership, office, investment or market-presence claim; always distinguish real footprint from target opportunity.
2. **One global gateway** — serve every audience from a single institutional identity without diluting it toward any one region, relationship or product.
3. **African roots, global reach** — the African origin is a signature conviction and investment thesis, never a ceiling on the mandate.
4. **Structure over storytelling** — present capability, pipeline stages and deal mechanics with investment discipline; the site is where serious opportunities are discovered, structured and built.
5. **Governed publishing** — every material claim has an owner, source and approval status, and the independent nonprofit identity stays clearly separate from the commercial group.
