# Homepage — surface brief

**Scope & mode:** The Home route. Mode: **Persuade** — the visitor decides and acts.

**Audience & job:** Seven institutional audiences weighted equally, led by the investor/DFI read of the thesis "Africa is Now". The visitor is a senior external decision-maker who does not yet know the group and must conclude, within a screen or two, that it is credible and worth a conversation.

**Action (working form, not a decorative link):** **BUILD WITH WATERLIGHT** opens a role-based route selector — Investor, Government, Financial Institution, Technology Partner, Project Owner, Joint Venture, Africa Market Entry, GCC Market Entry — which reveals route-specific qualification fields.

**Proof / content:** the two-way operating architecture (From Africa to the World / From the World to Africa) across the Identify → Originate → Structure → Mobilize → Build → Scale lifecycle; the eight platforms; the four honest footprint statuses. No unverified numbers, offices, partners, clients or market presence.

**Constraints:** evidence-led; no unearned claims; footprint visually distinguished from target markets; Waterlight Save Initiative kept separate from the commercial group; WCAG 2.2 AA; mobile-first; English at launch.

**Chosen direction:** **The Bridge Chart** — the group rendered as a working nautical/trade chart. Memorable moment: the live two-way route network that redraws as the visitor filters by footprint status.

**Unresolved:** deploy target; CMS/CRM; data-room owner and access policy; final legal entity chart.
